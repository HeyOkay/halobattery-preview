One ring says it all.

Each device gets its own icon. The ring fills with its charge and only changes colour when you should do something about it. Try it. Ring thickness and filled pictograms are in Settings.

72%
Device

  • Plenty left. The ring follows your taskbar: white on dark, black on light.

  • Amber within 10% of your alert level (20% unless you change it in Settings).

  • Red at or below it. You get one notification, and the next only after a charge.

  • Green while charging. The arc breathes slowly.

  • Faded while asleep. The last level is kept for 5 minutes.

      The app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch: choose More info → Run anyway. Check the files against SHA256SUMS.txt, read the public build logs, or run it from source.

      Device not listed? Send one report.

      Most new devices start as a diagnostics report from someone who owns one. It takes about a minute.

      Open a device request

      Not sure what to send? Ask on Discord.

      1. Close the maker's app

        Quit Synapse, G HUB or the web driver, then wake the device: move the mouse or press a key.

      2. Take a diagnostics report

        Right-click any Halo Battery icon and choose Diagnostics, or open it from Settings → About. The “No devices found” icon has it too.

      3. Open an issue

        Write the device name and how it connects, then drag diagnostics.txt into the comment. Replace MAC addresses and serial numbers with xx if you like.
